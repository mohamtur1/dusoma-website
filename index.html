Action Domains

5 Pillars of Grounded Poverty Eradication

Our AI model and field operations focus strictly on the direct levers that lift households above the multidimensional poverty line.

Pillar 01 • Food Security

🌾 Climate-Smart Agriculture

Protecting harvests from drought and erratic rain with indigenous agronomy.

  • Organic pest management (Neem, companion planting)
  • Zero-chemical weevil control using PICS hermetic bags
  • Drought-resistant tubers (Cassava, Sorghum, Cowpeas)
Pillar 02 • Water Security

💧 Clean Water & WASH

Ending waterborne disease cycles through community-level purification.

  • SODIS (Solar Water Disinfection) using clear PET bottles
  • Multi-layer bio-sand and gravel filtration
  • Borehole maintenance & Village Water Committees
Pillar 03 • Financial Inclusion

💰 Grassroots Microfinance

Building local safety nets through Village Savings and Loan Associations.

  • VSLA constitution design & 3-lockbox security
  • Mobile money micro-ledgering (MoMo, Orange, M-Pesa)
  • Emergency social funds to avoid predatory debt traps
Pillar 04 • Primary Care

🩺 Rural Health First Response

Preventative health and triage where clinical access is hours away.

  • Correct WHO Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) formulation
  • Malaria prevention & fever danger sign identification
  • Maternal nutrition and infant immunisation timelines
Pillar 05 • Human Capital

📚 Vocational & Offline Learning

Empowering youth with technical livelihoods without waiting for grid power.

  • Kiwix and Kolibri offline digital library deployments
  • Solar-powered study hubs & apprentice pairing
  • Agribusiness bookkeeping and market price monitoring

Sample Prompt • Clean Water
"Our well water has turned muddy after flooding. How do we purify it safely without expensive chlorine kits?"
DUSOMA ADVISORY ENGINE:
Step 1: Rapid Clarification (Moringa/Alum): Crush dried Moringa oleifera seed powder or a pea-sized piece of potash alum into the water. Let sediment settle for 2 hours.
Step 2: Bio-Sand Filtration: Decant clear top water through clean cloth into a 3-layer sand and gravel bucket.
Step 3: SODIS (Solar Disinfection): Fill clear transparent PET bottles. Place on a corrugated tin roof under bright sun for 6 hours (or 2 days if overcast). UVA + heat destroys 99.9% of pathogens.
